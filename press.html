Artist Bio

Kaisos is a self-released lo-fi music producer from Munich, Germany — creating ambient beats, chill hip-hop, and slow-tempo instrumental music since 2024.

It didn't start with gear or talent. It started with a keyboard, impatience, and eventually silence. Twice, Kaisos walked away. Twice, something pulled him back. The shift was a decision to slow down — to make something honest before worrying about what anyone else might think.

Eight releases in, the sound remains consistent: warm, imperfect, and built for the hours when everything else gets too loud. Every release is built for a specific kind of stillness — after a long day, before a hard conversation, or when the city gets too loud. Lo-fi gave the space to create without pressure. The music stays free.

Kaisos records and produces everything in FL Studio. All releases are fully self-released and independent — no label, no middleman.
