Built on honest restarts.

It did not start with gear or talent. It started with a keyboard, impatience, and eventually silence. Twice, I walked away. Twice, something pulled me back.

The shift was not dramatic. It was a decision to slow down, to make something for myself before worrying what anyone else might think. Lo-fi gave me that space. A genre that asks for presence, not perfection.

When I uploaded the first track, I almost deleted it. I left it up. That was the beginning.

“Progress, not perfection. And this time, I am not starting over. I am continuing.”
Kaisos
