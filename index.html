The story

Built on honest restarts.

It did not start with gear or talent. It started with a keyboard, impatience, and eventually silence. Twice, I walked away. Twice, something pulled me back.

The shift was not dramatic. It was a decision to slow down, to make something for myself before worrying what anyone else might think. Lo-fi gave me that space. A genre that asks for presence, not perfection.

When I uploaded the first track, I almost deleted it. I left it up. That was the beginning.

"Progress, not perfection. And this time, I am not starting over. I am continuing."
Kaisos
I

Where it started

A keyboard. High expectations. No patience. The instrument sat untouched for months before I tried again with FL Studio.

II

What went wrong

Creating for judgment before a single note existed. I was focused on how things would be perceived, and that made everything feel wrong from the start.

III

Why I came back

Slower. Simpler. More honest. Lo-fi asked nothing of me except to show up. No pressure, no comparison, just the sound and the feeling.

IV

Now

Making music at the pace of real life. Imperfect, evolving, entirely my own. Eight releases in, and this is only the beginning.
