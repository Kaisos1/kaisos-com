The story

Built on honest restarts.

It didn't start with gear or talent. It started with a keyboard, impatience, and eventually silence. Twice, I walked away. Twice, something pulled me back.

The shift wasn't dramatic — it was a decision to slow down. To make something for myself before worrying about what anyone else might think. Lo-fi gave me that space. A genre that doesn't demand perfection, just presence.

When I uploaded the first track, I almost deleted it. I left it up. That was the beginning.

"Progress, not perfection. And this time, I'm not starting over — I'm continuing."
— Kaisos
01

Where it started

A keyboard. High expectations. No patience. The instrument sat untouched for months before I tried again with FL Studio.

02

What went wrong

Creating for judgment before a single note existed. I was focused on how things would be perceived — and that made everything feel wrong from the start.

03

Why I came back

Slower. Simpler. More honest. Lo-fi asked nothing of me except to show up. No pressure, no comparison — just the sound and the feeling.

04

Now

Making music at the pace of real life. Imperfect, evolving, and entirely my own. Six volumes in — and this is only the beginning.
